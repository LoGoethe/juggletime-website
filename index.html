About

Welcome to JuggleTime — the circus experience that blends expert skill with joyful spectacle. Whether dazzling a festival crowd, lighting up a school assembly, or bringing laughter to a local block party, we deliver performances full of energy, elegance, and flair. JuggleTime Entertainment is located in Chattanooga, TN and serves the greater Southeast region and beyond with top-tier circus and variety event entertainment. JuggleTime entertainment is insured up to $2,000,000 in liability coverage through Peep Specialty Insurance.


About the Founder

Logan Goethe is a Chattanooga-based circus artist and the founder of JuggleTime Entertainment. What began over 16 years ago as a passion for juggling has grown into a lifelong exploration of performance and production, taking him to stages around the world. His work draws from juggling, partner acrobatics, clowning, physical theater, music, and dance, blending technical skill, creativity, and technical production. At the heart of it all is a simple dedication: sharing the joy and wonder of circus with audiences everywhere.
